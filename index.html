A SMALL EXPLANATION

The shape of
something missing.

Most drawing tools treat erasure as a return to blankness. Here, an erased gesture stays behind as an invisible current. Its direction pulls new ink along the path you removed.

  1. Make a mark. Draw a loop or a long curve.
  2. Let it go. Choose “Let go” and brush across it, or release all ink.
  3. Draw through the absence. New threads bend toward its memory.

Reveal the currents to see the hidden paths. Switch to “Without memory” to compare identical gestures with no pull. Older memories grow gentler as new ones arrive.

Undo restores the last action. A new sheet forgets everything. Save a study to preserve both its ink and its memories.
